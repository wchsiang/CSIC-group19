## Problem Grounding

- **Q:** Who specifically has the collaboration problem you are addressing? Name a real type of person, not a generic "user."  
  **A:** Team members in a group meeting.
- **Q:** What do they currently do instead of your tool?  
  **A:** Manual note-taking.
- **Q:** What would be observably different about their collaboration if your tool worked?  
  **A:** Team members can focus on the meeting itself, without having to spend extra time taking minutes manually, and sometimes missing some important information.
